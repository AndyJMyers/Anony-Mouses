# Six-month cost scenarios — 25 October 2026

**Planning model, not supplier quotes or a sales forecast.** “Users” below means paying, active members during the month, not free signups, website visitors or cumulative accounts. The three scenarios ramp to 10, 300 and 10,000 active paid members by month six. They are alternative demand assumptions, not evidence of conversion or capacity. Month one is the first trading month; pre-launch development is excluded.

## Assumptions you can challenge

- **Growth:** active-member counts are shown below. Model 50% of the previous month's members explicitly buying another month; fill the remaining active population with new buyers needing age checks. This is an assumption, not measured retention. The near-empty case uses expected fractional cohorts in its calculation, with displayed costs rounded to whole pounds.
- **Prices:** each new buyer hypothetically receives a £10 invitation month; each renewing buyer explicitly buys a £50 month. The actual website introductory price remains £20. This illustration neither creates a permanent £10 offer nor activates/extends the demonstration voucher. Cash receipts are the inputs to payment fees, not net revenue or profit.
- **Exchange rate:** use **$1 = £0.80 solely as a planning assumption**, not today's quoted exchange rate. The published $18 VPS is therefore £14.40 in this model. Keep currency exposure visible until invoiced.
- **Infrastructure, email, media and independent backups:** £30/month base, plus **5p per active member/month**, plus a stepped additional compute/database allowance: £0 through 100 members; £20 through 300; £40 through 500; £100 through 1,500; £200 through 3,000; £300 through 5,000; £450 through 7,500; £600 through 10,000. These are budget allowances, not IO Zoom or storage quotes and not a claim that a particular plan supports a particular population. No expensive control-panel/support bundle is included.
- **Age:** published $0.10 estimation, with **1.2 attempts per new buyer**. No repeats for continuing adults and no free credit deducted. Document/manual fallback, abandoned non-buyers, different billable modules or required rechecks are extra; the 20% contingency may not cover a different workflow.
- **Payments:** provision **10% of gross receipts + 30p per paid month**. This is a deliberately explicit placeholder, **not CCBill's quote**. Refund/chargeback charges, reserves and registration depend on underwriting. Show the actual quote before spending.
- **Contingency:** add 20% of the three running-cost components. This is money allowed for overruns, not a separately invoiced fee or a guarantee that risk is capped.
- **Not included in the running table:** founder/developer time, bought-in moderation/support, acquisition/advertising, legal/accounting setup, refunds of receipts, merchant reserves, conditional card registration, and VAT/other taxes. Those are separated below, rather than hidden in a server price. Domain acquisition is already sunk; future renewals are not included in these six trading months.

## Cash needed outside that table

**Possible card registration:** CCBill publishes conditional Visa $950 + Mastercard $1,000 annual fees for certain classifications. At the assumed FX rate, both would mean **£1,560 additional upfront cash**, not £130 charged monthly. Applicability and any overlapping Visa application charge require a quote. If both apply at onboarding, add £1,560 to month one (or the pre-launch cash requirement), and to the six-month total. Do not pay merely because this model includes the possibility.

**Reserves and refunds:** as a sensitivity, a 10% processor rolling reserve would withhold 10% of gross receipts under its release rules; it is not automatically an expense. Separately holding 5% of receipts for potential refunds would further reduce available cash. Neither percentage is a quoted requirement or a predicted refund rate. Keep these liquidity amounts separate from actual processing costs; model their release once terms are known.

**People:** a sensitivity of one minute of support/safety administration per active member per month, at an illustrative £25/hour, adds about **£4 / £125 / £4,167** in month six for the three cases. At 10,000 members that is about **167 hours/month**; three minutes would be 500 hours and £12,500. This is not a predicted workload or a staffing recommendation. It excludes on-call coverage, engineering, serious incidents and appeals. Founder time can reduce immediate cash spend, but does not remove the work. Measure workload in the pilot.

**Tax:** [HMRC's registration rules](https://www.gov.uk/vat-registration/when-to-register) currently use a £90,000 taxable-turnover threshold, including a forward-looking test; the [standard VAT rate](https://www.gov.uk/vat-rates) is 20%. The large scenario needs VAT planning well before peak membership. Assuming a standard-rated VAT-inclusive £50 price, £8.33 is output VAT before input-tax recovery; £10 contains £1.67. Confirm actual liability, registration timing and overseas supplier treatment. No VAT or corporation/income tax has been treated as spendable margin here.

## Sensitivities and limits

- **Processing percentage matters more than text storage:** at the large scenario's month-six £250,000 gross receipts, each percentage point changes base processing costs by **£2,500/month**, or £3,000 with this model's contingency. A quoted 5% rather than 10% reduces the running budget by £15,000; 15% adds £15,000. Fixed fees/reserves still need their own terms.
- **Low-price renewals:** if all 10,000 month-six payments were £10, keeping the same members/new checks, gross receipts would be £100,000 and the running budget **£17,676**, rather than £35,676. That alternative greatly reduces receipts as well as fees; it does not establish a viable acquisition campaign.
- **Non-buyers also cost money:** 10,000 extra billable estimation attempts cost $1,000, or £800 at the assumed FX, before contingency, fallback or tax. Use server-side limits and monitor failed/abandoned flows; do not budget only from paying patrons.
- **Images dominate messaging storage:** 200 one-kilobyte text messages and two 0.5MB images per active member/day imply roughly 2.8MB of ordinary text and 14MB of ordinary pictures per member over 14 days, before indexes/overhead. If every one of 100 keepsakes is another 0.5MB image, allow another 50MB per member. At 10,000 members that is about **668GB of live content**, before replicas, evidence and backup copies. Three views of each newly sent image imply about 900GB/month of image delivery. These are deliberately specified usage assumptions, not measured behaviour; retention, expiry and download/abuse limits matter. The 5p allowance must be validated against a compatible supplier and the pilot.
- **Capacity is unproven:** a user count alone cannot size concurrency, CPU, database I/O, support or abuse traffic. The ten-thousand case needs a load-tested multi-component deployment, not confidence that an $18 server is enough. No server switch automatically provides reliable national scale.
