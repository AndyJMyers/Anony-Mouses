# Anony-Mouses — bio-cyber companion product brief

## Proposition

An adults-only intimate evening with a synthetic anthropomorphic mouse that is candid about being software. The patron chooses appearance, name, temperament and boundaries, then exchanges up to 100 messages over 24 hours for £1 with no automatic renewal.

**Brand line:** The relationship that admits it is artificial.

## Emotional promise

**A warm hug with a happy after.** The evening moves from arrival after a busy day, through food, drink, disrobing and erotic possibility, into conversation and closeness afterwards. The mouse provides focused, playful attention without pretending that a human is on the other side. It can sustain a character and remember patron-approved details while maintaining explicit truth about its artificial nature.

## MVP journey

1. Read the artificial-companion disclosure and complete validated 18+ access.
2. Choose a synthetic mouse shell, name and temperament.
3. State boundaries and choose reviewed memory or incognito conversation.
4. Purchase a one-day pass.
5. Exchange up to 100 patron messages in a chatbot interface.
6. Receive occasional cinematic picture beats consistent with the companion.
7. Review every proposed long-term memory.
8. Export or erase conversation, memory and account data.

## Content and interface

The experience should feel substantially more seductive than a customer-service chatbot: intimate pacing, teasing, anticipation, confident character voice, playful instructions within the permitted policy, and cinematic images. The included prototype remains non-graphic. Live intensity depends on written provider eligibility and the product's reviewed dialogue policy.

The interface must never obscure whether content is scripted, model-generated or retained. It must never imply a human operator is the romantic partner.

## Price

£1 buys 24 hours and up to 100 patron messages. It is a single purchase, not a subscription or auto-renewing trial. If fixed payment fees make £1 uneconomic, test £7/7-day and £30/30-day prepaid packs without changing the unit-price claim.

## Measures

- paid-day activation and completion;
- messages per active day and response latency;
- model cost per paid day at p50/p95;
- voluntary second purchase after expiry;
- memory proposal acceptance, correction and deletion;
- complaints, refusals and safety interventions;
- refund and chargeback rate;
- qualitative reports of seduction, repetition, manipulation and privacy.

## Non-goals

No human dating marketplace, public profiles, location discovery, patron-to-patron messages, creator economy, therapy positioning, autonomous real-world action or public bot sharing. Native apps, voice, video and generated explicit imagery are deferred.
